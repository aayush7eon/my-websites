Recipe for Nepali Food

here is the recipe for some popular Nepali dish...

Dal Bhat Recipe:

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1 cup lentils
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Rinse the rice and lentils.
  2. Cook the rice and lentils separately.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté.
  5. Add turmeric powder and salt.
  6. Mix everything together and serve hot.

Momo Recipe:

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup meat (chicken or beef)
  • 1 cup vegetables
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Mix flour with water to make dough.
  2. Prepare the filling with meat and vegetables.
  3. Roll out the dough and cut into circles.
  4. Paste the filling in the center and fold to seal.
  5. Steam or fry the momos until cooked through.

Thukpa Recipe:

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup noodles
  • 1 cup vegetables
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook the noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté.
  4. Add turmeric powder and salt.
  5. Add the cooked noodles and vegetables.
  6. Mix everything together and serve hot.
